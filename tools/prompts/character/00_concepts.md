# 0. 시안 4개 (방향 고르기)

**첨부**: `art-src/characters/chulgeun/chulgeun_ant_turnaround.png` (그림체 참고 — 산책 모드 캐릭터끼리 톤을 맞춤)
**저장**: `art-src/characters/$key/${key}_concepts.png`

```text
Create a concept exploration sheet: 4 DIFFERENT design variations of a cute
mascot called "$name", in the SAME art style as the attached reference
(cute chibi mascot, thick dark outline, soft flat cel shading, subtle paper
texture, sticker-like finish). Use the reference for STYLE ONLY — do not copy
its species or outfit.

CHARACTER: $description

SHARED TRAITS (all 4 variations):
- VERY chibi: about 2 heads tall. Big round head, readable face with clear eyes.
- Exactly 2 short stubby arms and 2 short stubby legs, with a small visible
  gap between the legs.
- Simple bold shapes and few small details (it will be shown about 70px tall).
- Colors must NOT include magenta, hot pink or purple (the background is magenta).

VARIATIONS (single image, 4 equal panels in a row, all front view, standing,
same scale, feet on the same ground line): vary the face and one accessory.

BACKGROUND: solid flat magenta (#FF00FF), no gradient, no shadow, no floor,
no text, no labels, no numbers, no checkerboard.
```
