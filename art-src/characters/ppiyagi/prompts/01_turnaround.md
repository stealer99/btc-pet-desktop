# 1. 4면도 (기준 시트)

**첨부**: `ppiyagi_concept_pick.png` (고른 시안 한 칸)
**저장**: `art-src/characters/ppiyagi/ppiyagi_turnaround.png`

```text
Create a character turnaround sheet of the attached mascot "삐약이".
Keep the design EXACTLY the same as the attached image: same chibi proportions
(about 2 heads tall), same head, same face, same colors, same outfit.
- a big round head sitting on the body like a snowman, with the head outline visible where it overlaps the body
- exactly two tiny black dot eyes, far apart and slightly uneven in height
- exactly ONE flat wide pale-yellow beak
- two big pale-yellow duck feet
- two tiny hair strands on top of the head
- no ears, no tail, no clothes
Same art style: dark charcoal outline (#3a3a3a) of medium thickness, drawn with
slightly wobbly hand-drawn strokes. NO shading, NO gradients, flat plain white
fill. Keep the silly, dopey look: blank stare, nothing perfectly symmetrical.

LAYOUT (single image, 4 equal-width panels, left to right):
1. Front view
2. 3/4 view facing RIGHT
3. Full side profile facing RIGHT
4. Back view

RULES:
- Same character, same scale, same colors in every panel.
- All feet rest on the SAME horizontal ground line.
- Neutral standing pose, arms relaxed at the sides. No props.

BACKGROUND: solid flat magenta (#FF00FF), no gradient, no shadow,
no floor, no text, no labels, no numbers, no checkerboard.
```

## 확인
- [ ] 4면 모두 머리 크기·비율·색이 같은가
- [ ] 발 높이가 네 칸 모두 같은가
- [ ] 2번(3/4면)이 가장 완성도가 높은가 → 산책 동작은 **3/4면 오른쪽**으로 만든다
